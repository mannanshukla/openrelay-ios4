Shake. Decide. Repeat.

A Magic 8-Ball with a decision model inside.

Ask anything.

Type a yes or no question and shake. This 8-Ball actually reads it, and tells you how sure it is.

Pizza or sushi?

List your options, add the situation, shake. A winner, and the odds for everything else.

It knows your next move.

Rock, paper, scissors against a model that learns your pattern. Good luck.

Read before you send.

Paste a text. A meter from genuinely fine to openly angry tells you how it will land.
